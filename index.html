About

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Horticulture, Faculty of Agriculture, Pamukkale University, Türkiye. My work focuses on fruit genetic resources. I study the pomological and biochemical traits of wild and local fruit genotypes, postharvest fruit quality, and rootstock–scion relationships.

Most of my field work is carried out in the Denizli and Aegean region. I work on hazelnut, almond, walnut, pear, cherry, quince, rosehip and cornelian cherry, as well as minor species such as jujube, cherry laurel and wild pear. Recent studies cover phenolic compounds, fatty acid composition, mineral content and antioxidant activity of fruit genotypes.

I am open to national and international research collaborations.
