Levent Kırca, Ph.D.

Associate Professor of Horticulture

Department of Horticulture, Faculty of Agriculture, Pamukkale University
Campus, 20600 Çivril, Denizli, Türkiye

I work on fruit genetic resources. My studies cover the pomological and biochemical characterization of wild and local fruit genotypes, postharvest fruit quality, and rootstock–scion relationships.

Most of my field work is in the Denizli and Aegean region. I work on hazelnut, almond, walnut, pear, cherry, quince, rosehip and cornelian cherry. Recent studies focus on phenolic compounds, fatty acids, minerals and antioxidant activity of fruit genotypes.
