Research

Research Interests

  • Fruit genetic resources: selection and characterization of wild and local genotypes, and phenotypic diversity.
  • Fruit biochemistry: phenolic compounds, organic acids, fatty acids, minerals, and antioxidant activity.
  • Postharvest: fruit quality at harvest and during storage.
  • Orchard management: rootstock–scion relationships and irrigation.
