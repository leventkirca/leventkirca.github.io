Research interests and selected publications

Research Interests

  • Species: hazelnut, almond, walnut, pear, sweet and sour cherry, quince, apricot, rosehip, cornelian cherry, jujube, cherry laurel and wild pear.
  • Germplasm: selection, morphological and pomological characterization of wild and local genotypes.
  • Fruit quality: phenolic compounds, organic acids, fatty acids, mineral content and antioxidant activity.
  • Postharvest: quality changes during storage and processing.
  • Orchard practices: rootstock–scion relationships and irrigation.
